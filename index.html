Welcome

The Heart of the Philippines

The National Capital Region, also known as Metro Manila, is the country’s center of government, business and culture. It is made up of 16 cities and one municipality, including Manila, Quezon City, Makati, Taguig, Pasay and Pasig. Our package brings together its best historic sites, museums, food districts and waterfront views in one easy, well-paced trip.

Plan your trip

Best Time to Visit

Metro Manila is a year-round destination, but the weather changes how comfortable the walking tours feel.

Best
❄️

Cool & Dry Season

November – February

Cooler, drier days and pleasant evenings, perfect for walking Intramuros and Binondo. December is also festive, with Christmas lights and decorations all over the city.

Hot
☀️

Hot Summer

March – May

Sunny and very hot. Great for sunsets at Manila Bay and for cold halo-halo, but you’ll want sun protection, plenty of water and early morning starts.

Rainy
🌧️

Rainy Season

June – October

Frequent rain and occasional typhoons, though crowds are thinner and the city looks green. Bring an umbrella and keep your plans flexible.

Tips for Choosing Your Dates

  • Weekdays are quieter at Intramuros and the museums.
  • Start sightseeing early to beat both the heat and the traffic.
  • Book early for December and the long holiday weekends.
  • Check the weather forecast a few days before your departure.

Good to know

Travel Tips & FAQ

How long is the tour?

The tour runs 3 days and 2 nights, with hotel accommodation, van transfers, a tour guide and entrance fees included. Meals depend on your package.

What should I bring?

Comfortable shoes, sunscreen, a hat, an umbrella, a refillable bottle and a valid ID.

What is the best time to visit?

November to February is cooler and drier, ideal for walking tours.

How do I get around on my own?

You can use the LRT/MRT, buses, jeepneys and ride-hailing apps. The package already includes van transfers.

What should I wear?

Light, breathable clothes. Dress modestly when visiting churches.

Is it safe?

Keep your valuables close in crowded areas and stay with your group, as in any big city.
